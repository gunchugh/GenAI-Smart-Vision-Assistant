ℹ About Object Tracking

This module performs real-time multi-object tracking using YOLO11 together with ByteTrack.

Every detected object receives a unique tracking ID that remains consistent throughout the video.

This technology is widely used in surveillance, traffic monitoring, crowd analysis, autonomous driving and smart city applications.
